Text-Shadow
-----------

   RESOLVED: order reversed from REC-CSS2, shadows now defined to be
   listed from top to bottom to be consistent with 'font', 'background',
   and 'content' fallback syntax. CSS3 Text will contain the update.

CSS2.1
------

   RESOLVED: Issue 1 (clarify 'width' on columns) closed no change.
     http://csswg.inkedblade.net/spec/css2.1#issue-1

   RESOLVED: Issue 10 (Undefined handling of images without intrinsic
     sizes in backgrounds, content, cursor, and list-style-image)
     closed proposal accepted.
     http://csswg.inkedblade.net/spec/css2.1#issue-10

   RESOLVED: Issue 22 (clarify that display: none affects non-visual
     media) closed proposal accepted.
     http://csswg.inkedblade.net/spec/css2.1#issue-22

   RESOLVED: Issue 34 (clarify that auto top and bottom margins are
     not set to zero for abspos replaced elements) closed proposal
     accepted.
     http://csswg.inkedblade.net/spec/css2.1#issue-34

Selectors, expressions, and whitespace was also (very) briefly discussed,
but no resolution was recorded in the minutes.
