RESOLVED:

   CSS2.1 Issue 37 proposal accepted
     http://lists.w3.org/Archives/Public/www-style/2008Mar/0074.html

   Media Queries will be published as Last Call Working Draft as soon
   as the W3C publishing moratorium ends.

   ISSUE-35 (CSS2.1) proposal accepted
     http://www.w3.org/Style/CSS/Tracker/issues/33
     http://lists.w3.org/Archives/Public/www-style/2008Feb/0283.html

Other notes:

   - A lot of time was spent discussing how the chairs want to use
     Tracker to track the WG. There was an extensive discussion
     regarding the intent of the process, the concern over potential
     overhead for editors, and specific suggestion(s) to the process.
     See minutes (bottom) for the chair's proposal:
       http://lists.w3.org/Archives/Public/www-style/2008Apr/0397.html

   - The CSSWG charter was discussed, in particular the wording on
     expected participation time requirements (1-2 days per week)
     and on relationships with other W3C groups.

   - (CSS2.1) ISSUE-33 was discussed briefly, and the discussion
     postponed to the next week.
       http://www.w3.org/Style/CSS/Tracker/issues/35
