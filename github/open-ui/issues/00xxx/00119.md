In conjunction with the new[ landing page](https://github.com/WICG/open-ui/issues/29) we want to simplify & clarify the vision statement. I've had reports from numerous folks that are initially confused by the Open UI project but as I walk them through it - it makes sense and they agree with the effort. As such, I think it would be good for us to outline the key goal and the way in which we intend to get there. I had a phone call with Dan Fabulich who did a great job of distilling down our discussion into a very clear vision statement. He offered to give a rough draft a shot of adjusting the vision statement so this issue is to track this work.
