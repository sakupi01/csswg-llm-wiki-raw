## @melanierichards — 2020-09-24

Thanks @erikkroes!

You may be familiar with [TPAC](https://www.w3.org/2020/10/TPAC/Overview.html), but if not, it's an annual meeting of various groups within the W3C. In non-pandemic years, it's a chance for groups to get physically in the same room to hash out thornier issues, and also enables groups to have joint meetings to work on shared scenarios. Open UI will have a few hours reserved to meet online during this year's TPAC.

I was thinking that since there's a few folks who are interested in having documentation for their component libraries in Open UI, it could be neat to do a workshop at TPAC. People who are already in Open UI could do a short presentation on why it's useful to have more libraries documented, and then can quickly cover how to build the project, how all the schemas are organized, how to plug info about your component library in the right places. And then we can have an hour of working time so people can get started on their PRs, and have the opportunity to ask questions.
