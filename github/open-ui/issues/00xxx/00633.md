In today's telcon (2022-11-03) some technical issues lead to us needing to find an alternative platform to meet on as well as a discussion of how zoom (and discord) are not as inclusive of platforms as they could be. I suggested we use [Jitsi meet](https://meet.jit.si/) which is free, open source ([github link](https://github.com/jitsi/jitsi-meet)) and web based (not requiring / recommending specific client software).

Should we switch to this platform for future telcons? There were no issues and no concerns raised during the discussion about the platform.
