On top of the open `slider-orientation` questions ([csswg-drafts#11891](https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/issues/11891)), `<rangegroup>` needs answers for:
- **Track start:** where is it in `vertical-*` and `rtl`? This moves `::slider-segment(1)`.
- **Keyboard:** browsers already disagree on Up/Down and Home/End for vertical sliders. Shift+Arrow segment moves (#1511) inherit this.
- **Pointer:** nearest-thumb and segment-drag must use the block axis.
- **Labels and tooltips:** anchored defaults flip to the inline sides on a vertical track. Which side?
- **Source of truth:** can a child input's orientation differ from the group's? (Probably not: the group owns the track.)
- 
## Ask
The group's orientation governs all thumbs, and order, keys and caption placement are defined in logical terms.
