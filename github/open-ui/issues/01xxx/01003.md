Related to #908 but specific to `<area>` elements. These were left out of the original resolution: https://github.com/openui/open-ui/issues/839#issuecomment-1739848561 but I feel like they're close enough to anchor tags to warrant another look.

It's not immediately clear to me from the minutes why anchor was allowed but area not?

## @brechtDR — 2024-03-06

I do know some instances where interest invokers on area might come in handy 

examples: 
- Worked on some interior design projects in the past, where you could hover over parts of the images such as a chair, when you did, you saw some quick info about the item.
- Theme park maps where you could hover to see waiting times of an attraction

So yes, I'm certainly in favour of this +1
