## @domfarolino — 2025-06-03

Related but likely not all that useful: https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Reference/Global_attributes/accesskey.

I think my preferred approach here would be to keep this out of the scope of the initial menu elements proposal. While something like it would apply neatly to menu elements, I think it has a lot of "command invoking" utility beyond just menu elements, so I'd probably shoot for designing it as a more general primitive outside of our use case.
