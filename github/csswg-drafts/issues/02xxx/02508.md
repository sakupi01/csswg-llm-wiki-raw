The tooltip text for `<content-position>` in the `'align-content'` [grammar](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-align/#propdef-align-content) wrongly includes `'left'` and `'right'`. 

The tooltip text for `<baseline-position>` shows "`last | baseline | first`".

Both errors occurs in tooltips for those terms in various other places too.
