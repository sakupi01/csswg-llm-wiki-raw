The [`copy-into` property definition](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-gcpm-4/#propdef-copy-into) references `<content-level>` defined shortly after. However, the [actual definition of `content-level`](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-gcpm-4/#content-list) uses `content-list` instead of `content-level`.

Also, for consistency with other specs, the left-side of the equation should rather be enclosed in `<>`, i.e. `<content-level>`.
