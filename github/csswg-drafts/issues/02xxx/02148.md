`css-fonts-4` [section 2.1.1](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-fonts-4/#generic-font-families) begins by describing behavior for "all five generic font families", but then goes on to list nine such families. I would suggest removing the word "five" (twice in the same sentence), replacing it with "nine" (ditto), or explaining how five of the families behave differently from the other four.

Thanks!
