The original per-axis subgrid proposal https://www.w3.org/TR/2015/WD-css-grid-1-20150917/ had included syntax for local line names. The subgrid also receives the line names assigned by its parent. When both are provided, should the local line names be _in addition to_ the line names of the parent or should they _override_  (hide) the line names of the parent?

Having both is trivial to implement, fwiw. I imagine this as a flag on the subgrid, something like: `grid-template-rows: subgrid scoped? <line-name-list>?`, that would prevent its items (including further subgrid descendants) from seeing line names in ancestors of this subgrid.
