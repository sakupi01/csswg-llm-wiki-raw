The [`<feature-value-block>` definition](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-fonts-4/#font-feature-values-syntax) uses `{` and `}` without quotes. These characters have special meanings in the [Value Definition Syntax](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-values-4/#component-types) and should be "enclosed in single quotes".
