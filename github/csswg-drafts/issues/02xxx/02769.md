From https://lists.w3.org/Archives/Public/www-style/2012Apr/0101.html:

   - RESOLVED: Accept Anton's edit about margin-collapsing of a min-height parent and last-child for CSS2.1 bug 16037.
               https://www.w3.org/Bugs/Public/show_bug.cgi?id=16037

Previously fixed in [CSS 2.1 errata](https://www.w3.org/Style/css2-updates/REC-CSS2-20110607-errata.html#s.8.3.1c).

Originally reported by @dbaron.

## @gsnedders — 2018-06-15

Note later @dbaron sent https://lists.w3.org/Archives/Public/www-style/2015Feb/0189.html, leading to discussion on telecon https://lists.w3.org/Archives/Public/www-style/2015Mar/0185.html and:

  - RESOLVED: Change the 3rd bullet point of margin collapsing (CSS 2.1 section 8.3.1): Bottom margin of last              inflow child and the bottom margin of parent, no              longer collapse if the parent has non-zero min-height              and the bottom margin of the last inflow child              collapses with the top margin of the parent.              Exact changes pending more testing.

@fantasai took [ACTION-666](https://www.w3.org/Style/CSS/Tracker/actions/666) to propose an errata for this.
