## @tabatkins — 2022-08-29

Right, !important solely affects the cascade algorithm for the element the style is applied to; inherited values continue to act as normal regardless of what cascade origin/etc they were generated from. This is almost always what you want; the various inputs to the cascade (specificity, origin, importance, etc) are rarely what you want for determining *all* style resolution, or else you'd have to make all your styles increasingly specific as you target things further down the tree.

Since there's a trivial way to get this effect when you *do* want it (append `" *"` to your selector to spam it onto all children), I'm going to close this issue.
