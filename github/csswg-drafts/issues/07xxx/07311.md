Currently, fullscreen is [modal in Chromium](https://chromium-review.googlesource.com/c/chromium/src/+/810584) (via `inert`), and has been for the past 5 years apparently. But it's not modal in at least Gecko.

The fullscreen spec doesn't specify modal-ness. This issue is to track whether we should add it or not. See also #6965 .

<!-- comment id=1162412224 author=chrishtr created=2022-06-21T22:06:07Z url=https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/issues/7311#issuecomment-1162412224 -->
## @chrishtr — 2022-06-21

I think we should go one way or another and put it in the spec, because it will affect the behavior of the `:modal` pseudoclass.
