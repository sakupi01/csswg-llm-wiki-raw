It happens almost every day that e.g. https://drafts.csswg.org/css-contain-2/ just shows:

> ### Index of css-contain-2/
> 
> Name | Last Modified | Size | Flags
> :--- | :--- | :--- | :---
> [[Parent Directory]](https://drafts.csswg.org/)		 |   |   |  
> [Overview.bs](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-contain-2/Overview.bs) | Sat, 17 Sep 2022 10:54:02 -0700 | 88933 | -rw-r--r--
> [issues-2019-10-fpwd.html](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-contain-2/issues-2019-10-fpwd.html) | Mon, 11 Nov 2019 16:47:35 +0900 | 2868 | -rw-r--r--
> [issues-2019-10-fpwd.txt](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-contain-2/issues-2019-10-fpwd.txt) | Thu, 14 Nov 2019 03:52:56 +0900 | 275 | -rw-r--r--

So clearly drafts.csswg.org is borken, but it can still display that table as a fallback.

Can we replace this useless fallback with a redirection to https://w3c.github.io/csswg-drafts/css-contain-2/ ?
