https://drafts.csswg.org/css-grid/#subgrid-item-contribution

> The [subgrid](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-grid/#subgrid)’s own [grid items](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-grid/#grid-item) participate in the sizing of its in the [subgridded](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-grid/#subgridded-axis) dimension(s) and are aligned to it in those dimensions.

I guess it intended to say something like

> The [subgrid](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-grid/#subgrid)’s own [grid items](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-grid/#grid-item) participate in the sizing of its <ins>[parent grid](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-grid/#parent-grid)’ tracks</ins> in the [subgridded](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-grid/#subgridded-axis) dimension(s) and are aligned to <del>it</del> <ins>them</ins> in those dimensions.
