It appears that the images are all broken in the [current TR draft](https://www.w3.org/TR/2021/CRD-css-easing-1-20210401/) of css-easing-1.  I think it would be good to either (a) fix the images after the fact or (b) republish.

(I suspect this was the result of a mistake with echidna, given that [it was published with echidna](https://lists.w3.org/Archives/Public/public-tr-notifications/2021Apr/0002.html).)

<!-- comment id=1185768223 author=tabatkins created=2022-07-15T17:50:19Z url=https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/issues/7505#issuecomment-1185768223 -->
## @tabatkins — 2022-07-15

Specifically, the `bikeshed echidna` command automatically includes any files found in *subfolders* named `images`, `diagrams`, or `examples`, but doesn't include any files at the same level as the spec. (It's common for us to stash all sorts of things at the same level that shouldn't be part of the publication.)
