Currently no browsers support [image-rendering](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-images/#the-image-rendering) for video

But video has reasons to need image-rendering:

- video as background image
- video capture from canvas and needs to maintain caller rendering method when transmitting to other peers via webrtc
