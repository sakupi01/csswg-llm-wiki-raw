Agree. Even if the spec [defines](https://drafts.csswg.org/selectors/#the-universal-selector) it as 'a special _type_ selector' (which kind of makes sense, although might be confusing regarding different specificity), shouldn't its formal grammar be `<type-selector> = <wq-name> | <ns-prefix>? '*'` (with optional namespece token) instead of `<type-selector> = <wq-name> | <ns-prefix> '*'`?
