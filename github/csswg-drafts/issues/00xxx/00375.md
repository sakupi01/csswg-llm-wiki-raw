Flexbox has a step in the algorithm that explicitly accounts for the increased intrinsic size of a flex line due to baseline alignment of its contents. Grid needs an equivalent section, one that also handles track spanning.

## @fantasai — 2016-08-02

Tab points out this is handled in CSS Alignment: 

https://drafts.csswg.org/css-align/#baseline-align-self

> This effectively increases the margins on the box to align its alignment baseline with other baseline-aligned boxes in its group.

https://drafts.csswg.org/css-align/#baseline-align-content

>   This effectively increases the padding on the box to align the alignment baseline of its contents with that of other baseline-aligned boxes in its group.
