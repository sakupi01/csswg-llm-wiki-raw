People may need to use viewport units in a writing-mode-aware way, so probably we should add logical equivalents for `vw` and `vh`. They could be called `vis` and `vbs` or something like that.

<!-- comment id=220242248 author=bradkemper created=2016-05-19T06:53:18Z url=https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/issues/113#issuecomment-220242248 -->
## @bradkemper — 2016-05-19

I understand i for inline, and b for block, but what does s stand for?

I assume the dimension would remain constant based on the writing mode of the root element only, and not vary relative to the element they are used in?

<!-- comment id=220242696 author=upsuper created=2016-05-19T06:56:23Z url=https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/issues/113#issuecomment-220242696 -->
## @upsuper — 2016-05-19

`is` and `bs` means `inline-size` and `block-size` which are the logical equivalents for `width` and `height` in [CSS Logical Properties spec](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-logical-props/#logical-dimension-properties).
