The [changes section of css2-testing](https://drafts.csswg.org/css2-testing/changes.html) (or [its equivalent on the CSS2.2 WD on TR](https://www.w3.org/TR/2016/WD-CSS22-20160412/changes.html)) has a [change to canvas backgrounds date 2014-07-16](https://drafts.csswg.org/css2-testing/changes.html#s.14.2).

The **change description** does not match the change.  The change description says that the change makes the canvas background undefined, while the actual change to the spec makes the canvas background transparent.  I suspect transparent is correct, although I'm not sure.

Yes, transparent is correct.

The WG decided first (2014-07-09) that the canvas background was not 
taken from the root element, without saying what it was taken from 
instead. I proposed corresponding errata text that said the background 
was "undefined". The WG reviewed the text and decided[1] (2014-07-16) to 
replace "undefined" by "transparent". I updated the errata accordingly, 
but indeed forgot to update the explanation. I've updated that now, too.

Thanks for the review!

[1] http://lists.w3.org/Archives/Public/www-style/2014Jul/0299.html
