The [changes section of css2-testing](https://drafts.csswg.org/css2-testing/changes.html) (or [its equivalent on the CSS2.2 WD on TR](https://www.w3.org/TR/2016/WD-CSS22-20160412/changes.html)) has a [change to canvas backgrounds dated 2014-07-16](https://drafts.csswg.org/css2-testing/changes.html#s.14.2).

This change introduces a parenthetical sentence that should begin with a capital letter, but does not:
> (in CSS 2.1, that is the case when the element or an ancestor has 'display: none'.)
