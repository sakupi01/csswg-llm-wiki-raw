In section 6.2 of the current [CSS 4 Fonts draft](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-fonts-4/#low-level-font-variation-settings-control-the-font-variation-settings-property), it says,

"axis values greater or less than the range supported by the font, are ignored and therefore have no effect"

However, the OpenType 1.8 spec gives different guidance to OT applications: that values be clamped to the range defined within the font. See the normalization algorithm given in the "Coordinate scales and normalization" section of the [OpenType Font Variations Overview](https://www.microsoft.com/typography/otspec/otvaroverview.htm) chapter.
