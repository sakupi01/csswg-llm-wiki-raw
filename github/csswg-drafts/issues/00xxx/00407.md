In the [grammar](https://drafts.csswg.org/selectors-4/#grammar) section,

> `<id-selector> = <hash-token>`

with a note at the bottom:

> In &lt;id-selector>, the &lt;hash-token>’s value must be an identifier.

Apart from that the note is really easy to be missed. Why not just

```
<id-selector> = '#' <ident-token>
```

<!-- comment id=354650057 author=fantasai created=2018-01-01T12:08:52Z url=https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/issues/407#issuecomment-354650057 -->
## @fantasai — 2018-01-01

I believe the reason is that something that looks like '#' `<ident-token>` will be tokenized as a `<hash-token>` by the tokenizer before we get to the parsing stage here. But @tabatkins would know for sure.
