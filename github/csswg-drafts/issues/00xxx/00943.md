## @tabatkins — 2017-01-23

`:has()` is equally as problematic, from a perf perspective, as "parent" selectors.  This is why `:has()` is marked as part of the [static profile](https://drafts.csswg.org/selectors/#static-profile) - it's explicitly *not* allowed in stylesheets.
