The [changes section of css2-testing](https://drafts.csswg.org/css2-testing/changes.html) (or [its equivalent on the CSS2.2 WD on TR](https://www.w3.org/TR/2016/WD-CSS22-20160412/changes.html)) has [a wording change to section 10.7 dated 2012-04-11](https://drafts.csswg.org/css2-testing/changes.html#s.10.7).

Part of the old wording is also present in section 10.4, so it seems like the relevant part of this change should also be made to section 10.4.
