The [changes section of css2-testing](https://drafts.csswg.org/css2-testing/changes.html) (or [its equivalent on the CSS2.2 WD on TR](https://www.w3.org/TR/2016/WD-CSS22-20160412/changes.html)) has a [change to section 4.1.1 and other sections date 2015-09-05](https://drafts.csswg.org/css2-testing/changes.html#s.4.1.1e).

The description of the change should perhaps be clearer that the difference is that, when an at-rule is found in a declaration block, parsing of declarations resumes after either a {} or a ;, rather than only after a ;.

The current description is:

    Malformed declarations are handled differently when the start of the
    malformed declaration conforms to the syntax of an at-rule. In that
    case, parsing resumes not at the next semicolon or at the
    closing curly brace of the enclosing block, but immediately
    after that at-rule. This is expressed by adding the at-rule
    to the core syntax for rulesets, as shown below.

How about changing the second sentence from

    In that case, parsing resumes not at the next semicolon or at
    the closing curly brace of the enclosing block, but immediately
    after that at-rule.
to
    In that case, parsing resumes not only at the next semicolon or at
    the closing curly brace of the enclosing block, but also immediately
    after a block ({...}).
