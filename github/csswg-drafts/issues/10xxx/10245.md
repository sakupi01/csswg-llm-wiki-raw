Mediaqueries 3 has [one proposed correction](https://www.w3.org/TR/mediaqueries-3/#c1), which [successfully completed](https://www.w3.org/2002/09/wbs/33280/mediaqueries-3-proposedcorrections-2022/results) its [Last Call for Review of Proposed Amendments](https://www.w3.org/2023/Process-20231103/#last-call-review). We can therefore fold it in normatively and republish the REC.

It also has [one candidate correction](https://www.w3.org/TR/mediaqueries-3/#c2). [Tests pass](https://wpt.fyi/results/css/mediaqueries?label=master&label=experimental&aligned&q=mq-invalid-media-type-layer), but we haven't issued a Last Call for Review of Proposed Amendments on it yet. We should.

Agenda+ to propose:
(1) folding in correction 1
(2) upgrading correction 2 from candidate to proposed
(3) republishing

> When publishing an updated version of an existing Candidate Recommendation or Recommendation, technical reports are expected to meet the same maturity criteria as when they are first published under that status. However, in the interest of replacing stale documents with improved ones in a timely manner, if flaws have been discovered in the technical report after its initial publication as a CR or REC that would have been severe enough to reject that publication had they be known in time, it is also permissible to publish an updated CR or REC following the usual process, even if only some of these flaws have been satisfactorily addressed.
