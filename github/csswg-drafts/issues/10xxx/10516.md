"Add this to [Document](https://dom.spec.whatwg.org/#document).[[[resizeObservers]]](https://drafts.csswg.org/resize-observer/#dom-document-resizeobservers-slot) slot."

What is that Document? I assume the idea is to get that from a global, but which global?

<!-- comment id=2200592701 author=emilio created=2024-07-01T16:34:24Z url=https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/issues/10516#issuecomment-2200592701 -->
## @emilio — 2024-07-01

Gecko at least uses the [current](https://html.spec.whatwg.org/multipage/webappapis.html#concept-current-everything) global's document at the point of construction of the observer.

I think that's also what WebKit does given the `CurrentDocument` [here](https://searchfox.org/wubkat/rev/00b3d6a56f5149245b9e1501389ae169846ec240/Source/WebCore/page/ResizeObserver.idl#33).

Not quite sure what Blink does / what the ScriptState corresponds to there... @chrishtr do you know?

In any case, it seems `current` on construction is probably the most straight-forward answer here.

This is observable via callback order, cc @Loirooriol
