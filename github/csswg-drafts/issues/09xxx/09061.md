Lab and Lch color spaces should use Illuminant D65; not D50. The D50 illuminant is more commonly used in real-wrold/physical applications like "car paint" while D65 is used for digital displays.

## @svgeesus — 2023-07-13

You are correct that D50 is typically used for physical measurements, like reading a surface color with a spectrophotometer. Also, as you note, in ICC profile processing, where Lab is the profile connection space. 

Which is why it was chosen: for compatibility with industry usage. This was not an error.

Oklab, which improves on CIE Lab in many ways, uses D65.
