The headings in the [current CSS Anchor Positioning draft](https://www.w3.org/TR/2023/WD-css-anchor-position-1-20230629/) are inconsistent.

Examples:

* “Determining The Anchor” or “Finding An Anchor”—faux/naive title case (capitalizing words like “the“ or “an” in the middle of a heading doesn’t match [common title case guidelines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Title_case))
* “Security Considerations” or “Fallback and Automatic Positioning”—title case
* “Document conventions” or “Terms defined by this specification”—sentence case

Also:

* “Anchor-Based Positioning” vs. “Anchor-based Sizing” (note the different handling of “-based”)
