The css-box-3 draft has got some "black text on black background" sorts of issues, if you view it with a dark color-scheme preference -- specifically in info-panels.  Screenshot of what happens when I click on the "origin box" term in https://drafts.csswg.org/css-box-3/#valdef-box-view-box :

![image](https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/assets/426803/1e4dfec6-cb4a-4cb2-b67c-2b154ff1bedd)

This is in Firefox with a dark color-scheme preference selected in preferences.  The issue is also reproducible in Chrome/Safari on macOS with dark color-scheme in system settings.

## @dholbert — 2023-10-06

The issue seems to be that this particular spec draft's HTML has incomplete color-scheme css.  Most/all other spec drafts seem fine -- e.g. the [css-box **level 4** spec](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-box-4/), and the [css-borders-4](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-borders-4/) spec and [css-grid-1](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-grid-1/) specs render with a black background and light text when viewed in this configuration, and their info boxes seem to render with light text over a dark gray background, like this (which looks fine):
![image](https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/assets/426803/da2bad69-f3ae-4cc4-a5f3-0a8e6cb72393)
