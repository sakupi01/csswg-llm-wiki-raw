The animation types of [`flex-grow`](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-flexbox/#flex-grow-property) and [`flex-shrink`](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-flexbox/#flex-shrink-property) are "by computed value" and "number", repsectively. It seems more uniform to change the animation type of `flex-shrink` to "by computed value" as well.
