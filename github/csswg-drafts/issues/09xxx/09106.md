Don't you think that `!important` is often considered as a "code smell" that can now be avoided with `@layer` and `@scope`? And you can still increase the specificity of the selector whose declaration should be more important.

<!-- comment id=1648253065 author=tabatkins created=2023-07-24T16:42:08Z url=https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/issues/9106#issuecomment-1648253065 -->
## @tabatkins — 2023-07-24

`!important`, *in general*, shouldn't be heavily used. The [`@layer` rule](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-cascade-5/#layering) is the intended way to handle the sort of specificity conflicts that caused people to overuse `!important` in the past.
