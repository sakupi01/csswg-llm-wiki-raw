The [spec](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-text-decor/#propdef-text-shadow) says `none | [ <length>{2,3} <color>? ]#` but at least Safari and Gecko serialise `1px 1px black` as `black 1px 1px`.

So does Blink last I checked. Although actually Gecko and Blink serialize colors as their rgb() equivalents.

(Also, both Gecko and Blink fail to drop a 0px blur radius from their serialization which, as I understand it, should not be serialized since it is an optional component with its default value.)
