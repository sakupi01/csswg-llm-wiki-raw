When referring to the spec, under section 4.1 on Positional Alignment (on line 323 of the `Overview.bs` file), the last 2 values on the definition list are `left` and `right`.
> left (self, content)
...
> right (self, content) 
...

In the paragraph of grammar terms below the list, neither <self-position> nor <content-position> include`left` and `right` in their lists. Where does `left` and `right` apply?

<!-- comment id=343969722 author=SelenIT created=2017-11-13T16:14:10Z url=https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/issues/1980#issuecomment-343969722 -->
## @SelenIT — 2017-11-13

It seems that these values were removed from the spec [at some point](https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/issues/1403#issuecomment-311722607), but not from all places. I assume they should be removed from the list you refer to as well.

<!-- comment id=378433132 author=fantasai created=2018-04-03T23:39:00Z url=https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/issues/1980#issuecomment-378433132 -->
## @fantasai — 2018-04-03

They were not removed, they are merely restricted to `justify-self`, so we couldn't include them in the shared `<self-alignment>` grammar set. Remember that there is a difference between a non-terminal grammar term (which is just for convenience and readability) and a property's actual value space (which has a real-world meaning)!
