Step 2 on https://drafts.csswg.org/css-fonts-4/#fontstylematchingalg

If a font with a oblique angle of 21deg is requested, the spec currently states that first fonts with oblique angles of > 21 are checked, then fonts with oblique angles of 0 <= angle <= 21 are checked.

Instead, it should state that fonts with oblique angles of >= 21 are checked, then fonts with oblique angles of < 21 are checked.
