A [run-in sequence](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-display-3/#run-in-sequence) is defined as

> a maximal sequence of consecutive sibling run-in boxes and intervening white space and/or out-of-flow boxes.

There are various characters that may be considered white space or not depending on the context. I assume here it refers to what CSS Text calls [document white space characters](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-text-3/#white-space). I think a link to that definition would be useful.
