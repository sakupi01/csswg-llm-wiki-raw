['grid-template-columns' and 'grid-template-rows'](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-grid/#propdef-grid-template-columns) are animatable (as simple lists of length, percentage, or calc).

Thus the shorthands ['grid'](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-grid/#propdef-grid) and ['grid-template'](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-grid/#propdef-grid-template) should not specify discrete animation.
