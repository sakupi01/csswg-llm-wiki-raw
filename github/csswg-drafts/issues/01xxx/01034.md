https://drafts.csswg.org/css-fonts-3/#font-synthesis-prop

Currently, font-synthesis just takes `none | [ weight || style ]` That works fine for dealing with bold and italics, but there are other things that are synthesized that one may want to turn off.

At least the `font-variant-caps: small-caps` and `font-variant-caps: all-small-caps` seem like good candidates.
