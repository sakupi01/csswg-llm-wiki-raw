In [Snapshot 2017 §2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/2017/NOTE-css-2017-20170131/#profiles), the CSS Mobile Profile 2.0 and CSS TV Profile 1.0 specs are mentioned, but they seem to be “abandoned” according to the [“current work” page](https://www.w3.org/Style/CSS/current-work.en.html).

What are the current status & future of CSS profiles? Links to related discussions are very appreciated.

<!-- comment id=277595850 author=frivoal created=2017-02-06T06:24:38Z url=https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/issues/1006#issuecomment-277595850 -->
## @frivoal — 2017-02-06

The profiles have been carried over to the 2017 snapshot from the previous one without much thought given to the topic.

Personally, I don't think defining profiles is a terribly useful thing to do, and would be OK with dropping them from the snapshot for future editions, as well as dropping them from the 2017 snapshot and pushing an update, but that's just me.

Given that the profiles are not that old, it seems likely that some people disagree with me about their usefulness, or at least disagreed until fairly recently.

Agenda+ to see if the WG wants to drop the profiles from the snapshot, and possibly retire them altogether.

## @fantasai — 2017-02-07

The profiles in the Snapshot are there because other industries reference them in their standards, and they are therefore part of our spec corpus and not things we can drop. We list them as "abandoned" because nobody is working on them, and they are not being actively maintained.
