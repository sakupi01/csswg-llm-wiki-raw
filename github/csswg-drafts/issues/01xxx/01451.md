One of the sections on baseline alignment is called [Baseline Alignment Terminology](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-align/#baseline-terms).  But both the previous section, on [Determining the Baselines of a Box](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-align/#baseline-export), and this section, are a bunch of definitions.

What distinguishes this section is that defines which boxes baseline align to which other boxes, in other words, how they group.  I'd suggest renaming this section to something more like "Grouping of Baseline Alignment".
