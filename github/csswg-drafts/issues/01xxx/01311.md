> **Latest published version:**

But that URL gives a 404 error.

This also affects the bibliography of [CSS Flebox](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-flexbox/#biblio-css-writing-modes-4) and [CSS Grid](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-grid/#biblio-css-writing-modes-4), which have that same link.

If Writing Modes Level 4 has not been published yet, I think they should either link the draft or link Writing Modes Level 3.
