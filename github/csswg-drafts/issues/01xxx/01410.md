The definitions of the &lt;baseline-position> value in the [definition of align-self](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-align/#propdef-align-self) and the [definition of justify-self](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-align/#propdef-justify-self) both begin with:

> Indicates [baseline content-alignment](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-align/#baseline-content-alignment)

These should instead say:

> Indicates [baseline self-alignment](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-align/#baseline-align-self)

(note change of link)
