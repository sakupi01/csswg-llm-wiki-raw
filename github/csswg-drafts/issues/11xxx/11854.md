## @mfreed7 — 2025-03-12

One important thing that @lukewarlow [pointed out](https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/issues/11894#issuecomment-2711523217): there are **two versions** of the css-forms spec:

- https://drafts.csswg.org/css-forms-1/  (updated 9 March 2025)
- https://drafts.csswg.org/css-forms/  (updated 5 March 2025)

Both say they're "CSS Form Control Styling Level 1", but one is slightly out of date. E.g. link 1 contains something about [`::color-swatch`](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-forms-1/#color-swatch-pseudo) but link 2 does not. I'm not sure which of those I reviewed, but from the sounds of it, I reviewed the out-of-date second one.

It seems like it'd be very good to only publish one?
