Hiding due to the `position-visibility` property does not really relate more to [Anchor-Based Positioning](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-anchor-position-1/#positioning) than to [Anchor-Based Sizing](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-anchor-position-1/#sizing). So it's unclear to me why the [Conditional Hiding: the position-visibility property](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-anchor-position-1/#position-visibility) subsection logically belongs under the "Anchor-Based Positioning" section.

To my mind it would make more sense for the "Conditional Hiding" subsection to be a subsection at the end of the [Overflow Management](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-anchor-position-1/#fallback) section.

## @jwatt — 2024-10-12

My reason for suggesting putting it at the **END** of the [Overflow Management](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-anchor-position-1/#fallback) section is because the description of the `no-overflow` value makes reference to the `position-try` property already discussed in that section.

> If the box overflows its [inset-modified containing block](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-position-3/#inset-modified-containing-block) even after applying [position-try](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-anchor-position-1/#propdef-position-try), then the box is [strongly hidden](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-anchor-position-1/#strongly-hidden).
