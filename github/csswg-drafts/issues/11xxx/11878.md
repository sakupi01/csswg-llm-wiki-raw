In [Section 10.2](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-grid-2/#static-position), the [CSS2 reference](https://www.w3.org/TR/CSS2/visudet.html#abs-non-replaced-width) is not very relevant to absolutely positioned children of a grid container.  

Consider explicitly stating that the "static position" (of an element)  here refers to an abspos element with unspecified grid-placement properties.
