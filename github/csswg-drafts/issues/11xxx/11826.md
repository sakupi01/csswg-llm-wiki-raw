Given you can write `progress(100vw, 200px, 4000px)` and `progress(100cqw, 100px, 400px)`... do we even really need `media-progress()` and `container-progress()`?

`progress()` with viewport units even has the advantage of letting you choose among `sv*`,`lv*`, and `dv*`.

So are there use cases that require `media-progress()` or `container-progress()` or should we keep things simple and drop them in favor of `progress()`?

## @kizu — 2025-03-05

I think the only thing that `container-progress` currently provides: an ability to mention the container name, for which I would prefer https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/issues/7858 anyway.

Also, I don't know if it is a limitation of the current prototype in Chrome Canary, but by quickly playing with the `container-progress()`, I couldn't even make what I wanted with it, and it felt more verbose than with just `progress()`, even from the syntax standpoint.

I would be totally fine with dropping the `media-progress()` and `container-progress()`, especially given custom functions could allow us to experiment with the syntax for something more container/media-related, and if there will emerge a much more convenient pattern, we could always adopt it later as a built-in funciton.
