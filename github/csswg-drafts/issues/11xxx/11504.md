> The stripes defined by [<image-1D>](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-images-4/#typedef-image-1d) follow the shape of the border on the side to which they apply, and are drawn in bands starting from the [padding edge](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-box-4/#padding-edge) and progressing outwards.

What does outwards mean when this is a result of [collapsing](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-tables/#border-collapsing) 2 borders from adjacent table cells?
