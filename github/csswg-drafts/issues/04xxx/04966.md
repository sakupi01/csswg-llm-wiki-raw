I wonder if this bidi behavior is worth adding facilities for in CSS: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boustrophedon. Alternate lines have horizontally reversed characters, so the flow of reading flows back and forth across the page. It's sort of like specifying `<bdo>` on every other line.

(It's low priority, but maybe worth considering?)

## @Crissov — 2020-04-17

It has been mentioned before, but was not considered worthy of addition at the time. 

- [2016 Nov](https://lists.w3.org/Archives/Public/www-style/2016Nov/0031.html)
- [2004 May](https://lists.w3.org/Archives/Public/www-style/2004May/0350.html) 
- [2001 Feb](https://lists.w3.org/Archives/Public/www-style/2001Feb/0086.html) 

Historically, there have been variants with non-mirrored, horizontally mirrored, vertically and horizontally mirrored glyphs.

Yeah, since its usage is purely historical, with no living language using it, it hasn't been considered worth puzzling out the difficulties, and spending time speccing/implementing/testing it.
