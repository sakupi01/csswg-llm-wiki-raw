In [8.3.1 Collapsing margins](https://drafts.csswg.org/css2/box.html#collapsing-margins), the first use of the term _adjoining_:

> In CSS, the adjoining margins of two or more boxes…

should be hyperlinked to its definition a little further on in the section:

> Two margins are adjoining if and only if:

so that the reader immediately knows the term is being used in a technical sense and what its precise definition is.
