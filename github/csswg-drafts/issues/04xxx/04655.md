in [Color Spaces of Untagged Colors](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-color-4/#untagged), recommendations are given for video below 720p (i.e. NTSC- and PAL-esolution video), 720p, and 1080p.

There are no recommendations for 4k or 8k video.

They should probably recommend Rec.2020 for SDR 4k video.
