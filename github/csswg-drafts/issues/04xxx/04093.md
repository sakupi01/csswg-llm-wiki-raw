It appears the CSS Fonts 3 grammar
`normal | [ <numeric-figure-values> || <numeric-spacing-values> || <numeric-fraction-values> || ordinal || slashed-zero ]`
was accidentally changed when it was brought into CSS Font 4
`normal | [ <numeric-figure-values> || <numeric-spacing-values> || <numeric-fraction-values> || ordinal || slashed-zero ] titling-caps`
The examples do not have titling-caps, and browsers do not expect titling-caps.

The relevant change is https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/commit/b5beab956
