In [Mixing colors: the color-mix function](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-color-5/#colormix) and also [Adjusting colors: the color-adjust function](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-color-5/#coloradjust), the term &lt;color-function> is used, but is not defined.

The following functions are defined informally, by being used in the examples:

  - `lightness()`
  - `hue()`
  - `a()`
  - `b()`

I assume that this one will also exist

  - `chroma()`

When another colorspace than `lch()` or `lab()` is used, there will also be color functions for those spaces (`rgb()`, `hsl()`, `hwb()`).
