A survey of design systems shows that a vast majority round the corners of their input controls to make them not-sharp: usually by 4px, sometimes by 6px. We propose adopting this general consensus by adding `border-radius: 4px` to the base appearance for `input`, `textarea`, `select` and `::picker`.

```
input, textarea, select, ::picker(...) {
  border-radius: 4px;
}
```

There is also the question of what to do for buttons, if they should change to match this radius or continue to have a different, more pronounced radius. But there was more variation on this point, so that can be a separate discussion if we want.

## @stubbornella — 2026-09-09

In a design system survey I did recently, 25 of 33 design systems match the button, input, and select `border-radius`. So your instincts are spot on @Emilio. Most systems choose to make inputs, selects, and buttons all match one of these two values: 

- `4px` - Most systems, used by government and enterprise design systems (Fluent, Spectrum, USWDS, most governments)
- `6px` - Most usage, used by popular and growing design system libraries (Bootstrap, Tailwind, Ant, shadcn, Primer)

Either of those values would be a totally valid choice for buttons, inputs, and selects. Weirdly, no design systems chose to use 5px, so we should not take the average of the two. Personally, I'd weigh usage more and go with 6px. It has the added benefit of making the controls look more interactive.

Far fewer design systems choose pill, square corners, or to round the button more than the other controls. I have more data I can share tomorrow if folks are interested.
