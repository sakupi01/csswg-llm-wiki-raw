[UAX14, LB13](http://www.unicode.org/reports/tr14/#LB13):
> Do not break before ‘]’ or ‘!’ or ‘/’, even after spaces. 
> × CL
> × CP
> × EX
> × SY

But Chrome/Firefox does break.

Related: 
- https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/pull/9997
- https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/issues/9432

```html
<html><head><meta charset="utf-8"><style>
.m{width:min-content;font:20px/1.4 sans-serif;outline:1px solid #f99;}
</style></head><body>
<div class="m">x ] y</div>
<div class="m">x ! y</div>
<div class="m">x ; y</div>
<div class="m">x , y</div>
<div class="m">x . y</div>
<div class="m">x : y</div>
<div class="m">x ? y</div>
<div class="m">x ) y</div>
<div class="m">x } y</div>
<div class="m">x / y</div>
</body></html>
```
