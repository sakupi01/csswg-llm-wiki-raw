`calc-size()` can accept all the sizing keywords as its first argument, plus the keyword `any`. But what does `any` mean? It took me awhile (and reorganizing the spec) to figure it out.

AFAICT, it just represents a generic fixed size, i.e. a non-intrinsic size. Which is actually a pretty simple concept, but not one I would associate with `any`.

I suspect the naming came from the fact that it's _compatible_ with "any" other calc-size() type, but that's not what it _is_. Can we rename it to a keyword that better represents what it _is_, like `fixed` or something?
