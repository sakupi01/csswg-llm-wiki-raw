The scroll-anchoring spec describes a [suppression window](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-scroll-anchoring/#suppression-windows) which includes:

> or immediately before the next operation whose result or side effects would differ as a result of a change in the scroll position (for example, an invocation of [getBoundingClientRect()](https://drafts.csswg.org/cssom-view-1/#dom-element-getboundingclientrect))

This doesn't seem to match implementations; in every browser I tested, calling `clientWidth`, whose result does not depend on scroll position, closes a suppression window. Implementations generally seem to close the suppression window for anything that requires a forced layout.
