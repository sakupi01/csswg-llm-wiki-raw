### Context

The `normal` initial value is defined per container type: for **grid**, a decoration starts and ends at "T" intersections 
(it stops when it meets an item spanning across the gap) but continues through "cross" intersections; 
for **flex**, `normal` behaves like `none` (each line's decorations are continuous and independent).
Grid lanes containers are not yet covered.

### Proposal

- **Grid axis (like grid):** A grid-axis rule starts and ends at "T"
  intersections and continues through "cross" intersections. Concretely, it
  breaks where a spanner spans across its gutter, matching grid's behavior.
- **Stacking axis (like flex):** A stacking-axis rule behaves as `none`: it is a
  single continuous decoration along its gutter and never breaks at an
  intersection. Important to note that this means that the segments adjacent to
  a spanner will be joined.
