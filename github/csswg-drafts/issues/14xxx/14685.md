https://drafts.csswg.org/css-multicol-1/#the-multi-column-model

> [...] when a spanning element divides the multi-column container: the columns before the spanning element are balanced and shortened to fit their content.

https://drafts.csswg.org/css-break-3/#box-splitting

> When a box breaks, its content box extends to fill any [remaining fragmentainer extent](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-break-3/#remaining-fragmentainer-extent)

Now the issue: If a column spanner is inside another container (and not a direct child of the multicol container), should this container be extended to fill any remaining fragmentainer extent? There's nothing that suggests otherwise, but the implementations (at least Gecko and Blink) don't do this.

```html
<!DOCTYPE html>
<p>Test passes if there is a filled green square and <strong>no red</strong>.</p>
<div style="width:100px; height:100px; background:red;">
  <div style="columns:4; gap:0; background:red;">
    <div style="background:green;">
      <div style="contain:size; height:40px;"></div>
      <div style="contain:size; height:25px;"></div>
      <div style="contain:size; height:25px;"></div>
      <div style="contain:size; height:25px;"></div>
      <div style="column-span:all; height:20px; background:green;"></div>
      <div style="height:160px;"></div>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>
```

Rendered like this:

<img width="422" height="146" alt="Image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1ed42465-128b-403c-a434-ba1e11c7e32d" />

Spanners are treated specially in these engines, and when a spanner occurs, its container isn't extended to fill the entire column. Maybe just a plain interopable browser bug.

But then there's this:

https://drafts.csswg.org/css-break-3/#breaking-rules

> To guarantee progress, fragmentainers are assumed to have a minimum [block size](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-sizing-3/#block-size) of 1px regardless of their used size.

Meaning that if we are indeed supposed to extent containers to fill the column, there'll be a 1px gap above the spanner here:

```html
<!DOCTYPE html>
<div style="width:300px; background:yellow;">
  <div style="columns:2;">
    <div style="background:red;">
      <div style="column-span:all; height:20px;"></div>
      <div style="background:lime;">after spanner</div>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>
```

<img width="313" height="46" alt="Image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/cd749d16-7732-418d-becf-dbc6450f1930" />

And I suppose we don't want that?
