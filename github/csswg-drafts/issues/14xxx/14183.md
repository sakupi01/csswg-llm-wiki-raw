**Title:** `[css-borders-4] Editorial: doubled "the" in "get the border-aligned corner clip-out path"`

**Spec:** [CSS Borders and Box Decorations Level 4 — §3.9.4.2 Computing a contoured path](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-borders-4/#contour-path)

## Problem

In the *get the border-aligned corner clip-out path* algorithm, the step that defines `vectorFromStartToControlPoint` has a doubled article:

> Let vectorFromStartToControlPoint be **the the** two-dimensional vector (2 ⋅ clampedHalfCorner - 0.5, 1.5 - 2 ⋅ clampedHalfCorner).

## Suggested fix

Remove the duplicate word:

> Let vectorFromStartToControlPoint be **the** two-dimensional vector (2 ⋅ clampedHalfCorner - 0.5, 1.5 - 2 ⋅ clampedHalfCorner).

Purely editorial; no behavior change.
