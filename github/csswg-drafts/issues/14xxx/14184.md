**Title:** `[css-borders-4] "get the border-aligned corner clip-out path" references undefined `outerCorner``

**Spec:** [CSS Borders and Box Decorations Level 4 — §3.9.4.2 Computing a contoured path](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-borders-4/#contour-path)

## Problem

In the *get the border-aligned corner clip-out path* algorithm, the `offset1` and `offset2` steps reference a term **`outerCorner`** that is never defined:

> Let offset1 be the vector between originalCornerStart and **outerCorner**, normalized and scaled by startInset ⋅ strokeA.
> Let offset2 be the vector between **outerCorner** and originalCornerEnd, normalized and scaled by startInset ⋅ strokeB.

Every other step in the same algorithm refers to this point as **`originalCornerOuter`** (the algorithm's first parameter). `outerCorner` appears nowhere else and has no definition, so an implementer cannot resolve it from the text.

## Suggested fix

Rename `outerCorner` to `originalCornerOuter` in both steps:

> Let offset1 be the vector between originalCornerStart and **originalCornerOuter**, normalized and scaled by startInset ⋅ strokeA.
> Let offset2 be the vector between **originalCornerOuter** and originalCornerEnd, normalized and scaled by startInset ⋅ strokeB.

Editorial (undefined-term) fix; the intended value is unambiguous.
