An application of overscroll areas that I see a lot is this:

<img width="573" height="134" alt="Image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5860baed-7bb7-42a2-b7b1-f7345a151420" />

Here, the overscroll areas _(the archive button and the toggle read buttons)_ are not the `push` nor the `overlay` behavior, but they go underneath the content.

While we could require authors to `z-index: -1` the content and have them throw some other CSS at it to keep the overscroll area positioned on the edge of the overscroll container, I think it’d be easier for author to achieve this by means of a new keyword for the behavior.

Mirroring `overlay`, that keyword could be `underlay`.
