Hi folks,

I was looking for specifications that have no FPWD. This means they have not had a [patent policy exclusion opportunity](https://www.w3.org/policies/patent-policy/#sec-Exclusion), so their patent status is unknown.

(I left out Unofficial Drafts, indexes, templates like css-module, and jokes like css-egg)

## ED with no TR: metadata line (8)

css-extensions-1
css-flexbox-2
css-gcpm-4
css-navigation-1
css-page-4
css-shadow-1
css-variables-2
pointer-animations-1

_Flexbox-2 also has an odd "Changes since the 14 October 2025 CRD", copied from Flexbox-1_

## TR: link present but it returns 404 (7)

compositing-2    https://www.w3.org/TR/compositing-2/
css-color-6    https://www.w3.org/TR/css-color-6/
css-images-5    https://www.w3.org/TR/css-images-5/
css-overscroll-2    https://www.w3.org/TR/css-overscroll-2/
css-shapes-2    https://www.w3.org/TR/css-shapes-2/
css-size-adjust-1    https://www.w3.org/TR/css-size-adjust/
filter-effects-2    https://www.w3.org/TR/filter-effects-2/

Some of these have had significant work, and some have implementations. I think we should triage these into 

1. Should be published now, as-is
2. Waiting for near-term feature to be added before fpwd
3. Not enough in there to warrant publication, yet
4. Abandoned

I encourage editors to chime in if they have opinions on which category their specs belong to.
