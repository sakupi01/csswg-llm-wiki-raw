Currently, all text decorations have square corners. I have often come across cases where rounding is desired. Sometimes the lack of rounding is acceptable, even if undesirable. Other times authors have to resort to non-`text-decoration` solutions entirely.

This is more noticeable for thicker text decorations, with the extreme being [things like this](https://codepen.io/editor/sturobson/pen/01a13041-f4a3-7e49-a8b9-055b093efced/bfef2ac524a013e1d9ad38ba23b064de) where `text-decoration` is used to simulate a lading skeleton for text.
