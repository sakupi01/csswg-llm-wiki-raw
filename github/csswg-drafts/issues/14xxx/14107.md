There is [granular MDN data](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/Selectors/Attribute_selectors#browser_compatibility) for the case-sensitive/case-insensitive modifiers of attribute selectors, but the [relevant section of the Selectors spec](https://drafts.csswg.org/selectors/#attribute-case) lacks an MDN panel for this compatibility data.

Contrast: https://drafts.csswg.org/selectors/#attribute-selectors
