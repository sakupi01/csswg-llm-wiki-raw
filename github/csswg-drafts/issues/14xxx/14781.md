For `appearance: base` on listboxes (`<select size=N>`), we propose:
- Styling `<select>` itself (from the border outward) just like any other `<input>` or `<select>` in the page.
- Styling its internals (descendants and padding) just like the contents of `::picker(select)`.

This basically boils down to:
- Confirm that the `<select>`, `<option>`, `<optgroup>`, and `<legend>` styling for popup selects also applies for listbox selects.
- Except we take the padding, content alignment, overflow, and inner display values we decided for ::picker() and apply those directly to `<select>`.
