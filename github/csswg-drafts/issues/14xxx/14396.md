https://drafts.csswg.org/css-fonts-4/#font-relative

> The following properties defined in this specification are [font-affecting properties](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-values-4/#font-affecting-property):
> - All longhands of the [font](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-fonts-4/#propdef-font) shorthand
> - All longhands of the [font-synthesis](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-fonts-4/#propdef-font-synthesis) shorthand
> - All longhands of the [font-variant](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-fonts-4/#propdef-font-variant) shorthand
> - [font-language-override](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-fonts-4/#propdef-font-language-override)
> - [font-feature-settings](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-fonts-4/#propdef-font-feature-settings)
> - [font-variation-settings](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-fonts-4/#propdef-font-variation-settings)
> - [font-optical-sizing](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-fonts-4/#propdef-font-optical-sizing)

Problems:
 - `line-height` is a longhand of `font`, should it be excluded?
 - `font-palette` is not a `font` longhand, but should maybe be included? It matches the `font-*` pattern, though seems to only affect color and not metrics, so maybe not needed after all, unless color is considered a metric.
 - The `font-variant` longhands are already longhands of `font`, so they are duplicated
 - `font-language-override`, `font-feature-settings`, `font-variation-settings` and `font-optical-sizing` are already ([reset-only](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-cascade-5/#reset-only-sub-property)) longhands of `font`, so they are duplicated

## @svgeesus — 2026-08-28

> * `line-height` is a longhand of `font`, should it be excluded?

I believe so, but would like to hear other opinions.

> * `font-palette` is not a `font` longhand, but should maybe be included? It matches the `font-*` pattern, though seems to only affect color and not metrics, so maybe not needed after all, unless color is considered a metric.

Color is not a metric, and changing palette does not change anything else so the glyph geometry is unaffected. So it should not be included.

> * `font-language-override`, `font-feature-settings`, `font-variation-settings` and `font-optical-sizing` are already ([reset-only](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-cascade-5/#reset-only-sub-property)) longhands of `font`, so they are duplicated

Perhaps this is better handled by "All longhands (including reset-only longhands) of the " and then removing the duplicates.

## @tabatkins — 2026-09-11

Yeah, I just didn't realize *how many* properties were reset by 'font', lol. Fixed to make it clear reset-only are included, and dupes removed.

'line-height' needs to be excluded because it has special handling for font-relative units already, yeah.

And yes, I explicitly left out font-palette because it just adjusts colors, which don't affect the font-relative units.
