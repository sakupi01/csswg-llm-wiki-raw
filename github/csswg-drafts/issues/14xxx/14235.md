The current spec defines link parameters with a bare fragment:

`image.svg#param(--color,green)`

Fragment identifiers should probably be consistent and conform to STTF [syntax](https://wicg.github.io/scroll-to-text-fragment/#syntax), so I'd expect:

`image.svg#:~:param(--color,green)`

## @hober — 2026-07-28

Also, this isn't just about matching the STTF syntax, it's also to match STTF's behavior in terms of [what gets exposed to the page and how](https://wicg.github.io/scroll-to-text-fragment/#the-fragment-directive).

<!-- comment id=5120325801 author=tabatkins created=2026-07-29T16:00:07Z url=https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/issues/14235#issuecomment-5120325801 -->
## @tabatkins — 2026-07-29

Hm, my current design was just based on extending the existing [SVG fragment identifiers](https://www.w3.org/TR/SVG2/linking.html#SVGFragmentIdentifiersDefinitions), but that obviously predates the STTF syntax and its more careful concern for forward-compat and the scripting interface.

I'm okay with switching to relying on STTF syntax.
