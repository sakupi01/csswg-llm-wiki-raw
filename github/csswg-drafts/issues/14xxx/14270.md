I'm fixing some bugs in the contoured path rendering #14142 , and I had to make an arbitrary decision about how to apply the outset-adjusted radius to offset shapes that are not `round`.

Basically, I'm extracting the adjusted outset from `outset_adjusted_border_radius` (because `adjustedRadius = radius + adjustedOutset`) and drawing the corner as the offset of a virtual corner with the same radius as the original corner, positioned at a distance of `adjustedOutset` from the outset rectangle.

The issue is that I'm not sure "adjusted radius" is a good geometric abstraction for arbitrary corner shapes. The algorithm inside `outset_adjusted_border_radius` was likely designed around round corners and does not necessarily generalize well to other shapes.

I would like feedback on whether my current approach is acceptable, or whether another generalization would make more sense.

The compatibility requirements are that `round` corners keep their current outset-adjusted behavior and that the transition between `round` corners and other corner shapes is continuous.

For example, one possible alternative would be to use the adjusted outset (as described above) for `corner-shape >= 1` and the normal geometric outset for `corner-shape <= 0`, with a linear transition between them.
