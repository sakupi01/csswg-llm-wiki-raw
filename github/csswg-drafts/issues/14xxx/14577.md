While `css-overflow-5` introduced the two scroll buttons (`::scroll-button(prev)` and `::scroll-button(next)`), an author needs to position them individually. When trying to position them as a group, this requires the author to know the size of each buttons. E.g. in https://chrome.dev/carousel/vertical/stack/ the buttons are translated by `55%` from their original position.

If there was a new pseudo that wraps both the `::scroll-button(prev)` and `::scroll-button(next)`, users could easily position them as a group, and even use layouts like flex/grid on that new pseudo to position the buttons inside of it.
