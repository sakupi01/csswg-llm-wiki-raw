The [current syntax](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-values-5/#funcdef-progress) is `progress(no-clamp? <calc-sum>, <calc-sum>, <calc-sum>)`.

This seems inconsistent with [`round(<rounding-strategy>? , ...)`](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-values-4/#funcdef-round), [`random(<random-key>? , ...)`](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-values-5/#funcdef-random), perhaps others?
