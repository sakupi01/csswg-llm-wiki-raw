In #12812 we resolved that `line-clamp` does fragment inside fragmentainers. However, what happens to the hidden content?

Most boxes and line boxes (but not all, see below) after the clamp point become invisible, and the automatic size of the line-clamp container ignores such boxes. Therefore, in most cases, such invisible boxes overflow the line-clamp container, counting as ink overflow. This creates a parallel fragmentation flow, and if there's no more content in other flows of the fragmentainer, then the hidden content might result in empty fragments. At least for pages, css-page discourages this.

We could treat line-clamp containers as not causing a parallel flow somehow. But this doesn't take into account the fact that some content inside the line-clamp container can overflow it while being visible. This is the case for relpos boxes, but also for abspos whose container block is not after clamp. And if the container doesn't end up actually clamping, this can also be true for floats which are clipped after overflowing the line-clamp container (see #13669). What happens with these boxes?
