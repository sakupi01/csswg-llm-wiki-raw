**Where:** CSS Cascading and Inheritance Level 6, Editor's Draft 28 April 2026, §3.6 Precedence of Non-CSS Presentational Hints (#preshint). The Working Draft of 6 September 2024 has the same pointer in §2.6.

**Text.** The section has no text of its own, only an issue box that points to Level 5:

> CSS Cascading 5 § 6.4 Cascade Layers

In the Bikeshed source this is `Issue: [[css-cascade-5#layering]]` (css-cascade-6/Overview.bs, line 929, under `<h3 id="preshint">`).

**What is wrong.** In Level 5, presentational hints are §6.5 Precedence of Non-CSS Presentational Hints (#preshint), not §6.4 Cascade Layers (#layering). The other pointers of the same kind in Level 6 go to the matching Level 5 section: §3.2 to §6.2, §3.3 to §6.3, §3.4 to §6.4, §3.4.1 to §6.4.3. Only §3.6 points one section too early. Level 5 also moved presentational hints out of layers into their own origin (#6659), so §6.4 is not where a reader should look for them.

No example is needed; the text itself is the defect.

Found during an AI-assisted review of the cascade specs.

**Request:** could the pointer in §3.6 be changed to `[[css-cascade-5#preshint]]`?
