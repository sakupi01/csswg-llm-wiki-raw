Seems to be a contradiction between [section 2. Overview of Alignment properties](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-align/#overview) that says that `justify-content` applies to block containers and [section 5.1. The justify-content and align-content Properties](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-align/#align-justify-content) that not says that `justify-content` applies to block containers.
Experimentation in Firefox and Chromium demonstrates the second proposition is right.
