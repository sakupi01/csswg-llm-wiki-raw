Two comments about the Arabic-script part of Example10 at [§5.2 ](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-text-3/#word-break-property)(https://drafts.csswg.org/css-text-3/#word-break-property)

1- The WAW letter و in "و کمی" is normally attached to it's next word کمی. There shouldn't be a space (nor a break opportunity) between them.
2- The Arabic translation should be وكذلك instead of وکمی.
