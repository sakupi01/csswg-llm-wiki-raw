Hello,
I'm in the process of implementing overflow-clip-margin in chrome and a question has come up as to what overflow-clip-margin applies to. Does it only impact the paint effects of the box itself? What about child paint effects and child spill out?

<!-- comment id=709628942 author=sky2 created=2020-10-15T22:45:22Z url=https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/issues/5620#issuecomment-709628942 -->
## @sky2 — 2020-10-15

We are planning on doing the following:
1. box decorations of overflow:clip element remain as-is and are not clipped in presence of overflow-clip-margin
2. area of descendants exposed by overflow-clip-margin contribute to layout overflow of ancestor

<!-- comment id=710700661 author=tabatkins created=2020-10-16T23:51:59Z url=https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/issues/5620#issuecomment-710700661 -->
## @tabatkins — 2020-10-16

overflow-clip-margin doesn't affect anything on its own; it just expands the [overflow clip box](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-overflow-3/#overflow-clip-edge), which is used by `overflow: clip` and `contain: paint`.

Those latter properties define how they interact with paint and geometry.
