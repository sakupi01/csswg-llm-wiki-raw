[`CSSImportRule`](https://drafts.csswg.org/cssom-1/#cssimportrule) has `.media`, `.layerName`, `.supportsText`, but nothing for `scope | scope(<scope-start> | <scope-boundaries>)`. Should it?

I guess the options would be a single attribute as a string, or two as strings or immutable lists of elements.
