> The following features are at-risk, and may be dropped during the CR period: 
> - the [revert-layer](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-cascade-5/#valdef-all-revert-layer) keyword

It has shipped on all browsers, so probably unnecessary.

On the other hand, https://drafts.csswg.org/css-cascade-5/#revert-rule-keyword is much newer, only implemented in Blink, and not enabled in stable yet.
