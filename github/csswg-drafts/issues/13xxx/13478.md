Could [css-sizing-3](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-sizing-3) please introduce something like `<box-length>` or `<box-dimension> = <length-percentage [0,∞]> | min-content | max-content | fit-content(<length-percentage [0,∞]>) | <calc-size()>`?

With that production, `<'width'>`, `<'height'>`, `<'min-width'>`, `<'min-height'>` could be simplified to `auto | <box-dimension>`, whereas `<'max-width'>`, `<'max-height'>` could become `none | <box-dimension>`. This would make the difference more noticeable. 

[css-sizing-4](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-sizing-4/#sizing-values) can then add `stretch | fit-content | contain` to it.

[css-logical](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-logical-1) refers to `<'width'>` and `<'min-width'>` and `<'max-width'>`. Although this is the future-safe way, it feels a bit weird that the _height_ properties also use a _width_ value.

[css-flexbox](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-flexbox/#flex-basis-property) could either keep its `content | <'width'>` value syntax for the `flex-basis` property, or switch to `auto | content | <box-dimension>`.

There may be other places affected.

## @cdoublev — 2026-09-04

The ec88f45 rewrite appears to have many problems. Below are the property value definitions (ignoring those from CSS Logical) before/after, with expanded `<box-size>` and concatenated `New values`.

`block-size`, `inline-size`, `min-block-size`, `min-inline-size`

```diff
- auto | <length-percentage [0,∞]> | contain | min-content | max-content | stretch | fit-content | fit-content(<length-percentage [0,∞]>) | <calc-size()> | <anchor-size()>
+ auto | <length-percentage> | contain | min-content | max-content | stretch | fit-content | fit-content() | calc-size()
```

`height`, `width`, `min-height`, `min-width`

```diff
- auto | <length-percentage [0,∞]> | contain | min-content | max-content | stretch | fit-content | fit-content(<length-percentage [0,∞]>) | <calc-size()> | <anchor-size()>
+ auto | <length-percentage> | contain | contain | min-content | max-content | stretch | fit-content | fit-content() | fit-content(<length-percentage [0,∞]>) | calc-size() | <anchor-size()>
```

`column-width`

```diff
- auto | <length [0,∞]> | min-content | max-content | fit-content(<length-percentage>)
+ auto | <length [0,∞]> | <length-percentage> | contain | min-content | max-content | stretch | fit-content | fit-content() | calc-size()
```

`max-block-size`, `max-inline-size`

```diff
- none | <length-percentage [0,∞]> | contain | min-content | max-content | stretch | fit-content | fit-content(<length-percentage [0,∞]>) | <calc-size()> | <anchor-size()>
+ none | <length-percentage> | contain | min-content | max-content | stretch | fit-content | fit-content() | calc-size()
```

`max-height`, `max-wifth`

```diff
- none | <length-percentage [0,∞]> | contain | min-content | max-content | stretch | fit-content | fit-content(<length-percentage [0,∞]>) | <calc-size()> | <anchor-size()>
+ none | <length-percentage> | contain | contain | min-content | max-content | stretch | fit-content | fit-content() | fit-content(<length-percentage [0,∞]>) | calc-size()
```

Note that `calc-size()` in the RHS of a production represents a function taking no argument.
