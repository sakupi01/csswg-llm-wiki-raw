At first reading, it is difficult to understand the difference between [Pause properties](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-speech-1/#pause-props), and [Rest properties](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-speech-1/#rest-props). 
At second reading, it is difficult to understand why they were not grouped under a _Break_ section, as there difference in definition is so tiny (including clue or not in the box model).

It would be more pedagogical for everyone: 
- those who do not know SSML ;
- those who know SSML with its `data-ssml-break-strength `and `data-ssml-break-time `properties; with cues requiring `<empty> `elements to associate an audio file to them.
