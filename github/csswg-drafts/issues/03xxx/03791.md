The [`:nth()` page selector](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-gcpm/#document-page-selectors) has no specificity defined. The specificity related to page names and pseudo-classes are defined in [css-page-3](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-page-3/#cascading-and-page-context), but doesn't mention the `:nth()` pseudo-class.
