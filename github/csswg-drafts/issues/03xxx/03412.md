According to SVG2, `overflow` applies to [elements which establish a new SVG viewport](https://svgwg.org/svg2-draft/coords.html#ElementsThatEstablishViewports), `pattern` elements and `marker` elements.

## @frivoal — 2021-07-28

We don't generally list SVG elements in our "Applies to" line. It may not be a bad idea to start doing it, but then we should be consistent throughout our specs. I suggest opening a separate issues to discuss the general approach.
