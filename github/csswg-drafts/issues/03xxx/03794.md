## @frivoal — 2019-04-03

No, this is not intentional. Level 4 is somewhat under-maintained at the moment, and all the stuff that relates to line-breaking and white-space processing is likely out of sync with the content of level 3.

Thanks for pointing it out, this is something we ought to address before long. There are a couple of open issues on that general area on Level 3, but once they're settled, we should make sure Level 4 is consistent with what we ended up with.

## @fantasai — 2023-03-21

Edited in https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/pull/8274/commits/568816e58bbcdc8e788fbad480f191179a58ee45 (see https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/pull/8274) ; Agenda+ for WG review. The proposal is to add `break-spaces` to `white-space-collapse`.

Pros: Mostly about how to handle preserved spaces.
Cons: The name implies that the spaces are breakable, but they're not unless wrapping is also turned on.
