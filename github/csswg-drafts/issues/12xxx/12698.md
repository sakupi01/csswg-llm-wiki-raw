## @svgeesus — 2025-12-02

This turned out to be more complicated than anticipated, because the bikeshed boilerplate rules for fxtf are unmaintained and very out of date. The checker even said that the two editors were not in the group, among many other errors.

We already resolved some years ago to move content from fxtf into csswg repo, so I found a [magic set of incantations](https://rdiachenko.com/posts/tools/git/moving-git-repo-files-preserving-change-history/) which achieves this. The geomery module [now lives in csswg repo](https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/tree/main/geometry) and has an [intact revision history](https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/commits/main/geometry).

I was then able to bikeshed it so it passed pubrules as a CRD: expected Thurs 4 Dec.
