Given the [huge list of features that are seem to be ready to release](https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/issues/12797), I wonder whether we should try to get Selectors 4 in shape for moving to Candidate Recommendation status.

To do that, I suggest moving the following features to [level 5 of the spec.](https://drafts.csswg.org/selectors-5/) and feature-freeze level 4:

* `:local-link` (already there, though needs to be removed from level 4)
* `:interest-source` and `:interest-target`
* `:blank`
* All grid-structural selectors, i.e. the `||` combinator, `:nth-col()`, and `:nth-last-col()`
* All time-dimensional pseudo-classes , i.e. `:current`, `:current()`, `:past`, and `:future`

@fantasai @tabatkins What's your opinion?

Sebastian

> [This change](https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/commit/a407bba4a8769998e498f9e25fb5c2d9e10651f5) lost the point that you can match invalid language tags, as long as they're well-formed. Was that intentional?

Yes. @aphillips [said](https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/issues/3022#issuecomment-4568519381)

> > We _can_ forbid non-valid tags/ranges from ever matching. The text for that might be:
> 
> > The element’s [content language](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-text-4/#content-language) matches a [language range](https://drafts.csswg.org/selectors-4/#language-range) if its content language, as represented in BCP 47 syntax, matches the given language range in an extended filtering operation per [[RFC4647]](https://drafts.csswg.org/selectors-4/#biblio-rfc4647) Matching of Language Tags (section 3.3.2). The content language and the language range must be canonicalized and converted to extlang form as per section 4.5 of [[RFC5646]](https://drafts.csswg.org/selectors-4/#biblio-rfc5646) prior to the extended filtering operation; language tags or ranges that are not [valid](https://www.w3.org/TR/i18n-glossary/#dfn-valid) do not match anything. The matching is performed [ASCII case-insensitively](https://infra.spec.whatwg.org/#ascii-case-insensitive).
