Given the [huge list of features that are seem to be ready to release](https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/issues/12797), I wonder whether we should try to get Selectors 4 in shape for moving to Candidate Recommendation status.

To do that, I suggest moving the following features to [level 5 of the spec.](https://drafts.csswg.org/selectors-5/) and feature-freeze level 4:

* `:local-link` (already there, though needs to be removed from level 4)
* `:interest-source` and `:interest-target`
* `:blank`
* All grid-structural selectors, i.e. the `||` combinator, `:nth-col()`, and `:nth-last-col()`
* All time-dimensional pseudo-classes , i.e. `:current`, `:current()`, `:past`, and `:future`

@fantasai @tabatkins What's your opinion?

Sebastian
