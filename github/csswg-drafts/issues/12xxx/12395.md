Currently, the name of the CSS-mixins module is "CSS Functions and Mixins Module."
https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/blob/cc8b65255773ac0ce819a362260043cf75bb01db/css-mixins-1/Overview.bs#L2

However, the term "CSS function" can also refer to other built-in functions such as colors and mathematical functions. Therefore, referring to `@function` as simply "CSS function" can be misleading. I suggest adding "custom" to the name of the specification and referring to it as "Custom Functions and Mixins" would be more clear to readers.
