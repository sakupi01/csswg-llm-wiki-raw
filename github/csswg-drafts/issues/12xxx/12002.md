The [spec](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-highlight-api-1/#dom-highlightregistry-highlightsfrompoint:~:text=descending%20order%20of%20priority%2C) currently mentions that highlights returned from HighlightsFromPoint() are returned by priority in descending order, but don't mention ordering for highlights with the same priority. The [tests](https://github.com/web-platform-tests/wpt/blob/3863a5a4ca3f98ce6ad39a88d929207d21dbf5a1/css/css-highlight-api/HighlightRegistry-highlightsFromPoint.html#L72-L73) imply that in this case the highlights should be ordered by insertion order descending.

## @schenney-chromium — 2025-03-25

I believe the highlights are returned in reverse paint order (i.e. front to back), where the paint order is increasing priority with ties broken by insertion order (later insertions paint after earlier). So maybe the spec text should just say "reverse paint order" and link to the paint order spec.

I think this can just be an editor change, though others may disagree.

## @ffiori — 2025-03-25

Hi @jnjaeschke, @schenney-chromium, thanks for your comments. From the spec for custom highlights, in the section that is linked to in highlightsFromPoint spec, [Priority of Overlapping Highlights](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-highlight-api-1/#priorities), it's specified which highlight has a higher effective priority if they're tied on their attribute value:

"When two or more [custom highlights](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-highlight-api-1/#custom-highlight) have the same numerical priority, the one most recently [registered](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-highlight-api-1/#registered) has the higher effective [priority](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-highlight-api-1/#priority)."
