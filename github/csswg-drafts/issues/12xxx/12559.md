Step 3 of [`serialize a CSSVariableReferenceValue`](https://drafts.css-houdini.org/css-typed-om/#serialize-a-cssvariablereferencevalue) appends a comma _and a space_ before appending the serialized fallback.
> If this’s [fallback](https://drafts.css-houdini.org/css-typed-om/#dom-cssvariablereferencevalue-fallback) internal slot is not null, append ", " to s, then serialize the [fallback](https://drafts.css-houdini.org/css-typed-om/#dom-cssvariablereferencevalue-fallback) internal slot and append it to s.

This is in contrast with implementations and tests (WebKit, Blink and WPT) that expects only a comma to be appended.
