Splitting the discussion from #9770 for adding [CSS Shapes 1](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-shapes-1/) to the Reliable Candidate Recommendations section of the CSS Snapshot 2025.

Data to consider:

Current status: CRD (last published: 12 June 2025), ED from 11 June 2025
[WPT](https://wpt.fyi/results/css/css-shapes?label=master&label=stable&aligned): passing tests: Chrome: 5337, Edge: 5258, Firefox: 5946, Safari: 5111; [interop](https://wpt.fyi/results/css/css-shapes?label=master&label=stable&aligned&q=all(status%3Apass)): 4637 / 6071 (76.4%), 5246 / 6071 (86.4%) for [Chrome and Firefox](https://wpt.fyi/results/css/css-shapes?label=master&label=stable&aligned&q=chrome%3Apass+and+firefox%3Apass)
Current Snapshot section: Fairly Stable
Open issues: [on GitHub](https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/issues?q=is%3Aopen+is%3Aissue+label%3Acss-shapes-1): 20 (~11 substantial for that spec. level), [in spec.](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-shapes-1/#issues-index): 0

Summary: High interoperability, many open issues.

Note: Due to the amount of open issues, it might also be put to Rough Interoperability, instead, though it probably should be removed from Fairly Stable.
