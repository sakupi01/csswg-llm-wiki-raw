In [5.1. Interpolating colors based on headroom ](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-color-hdr/#headroom-interpolation) is a note:

> Note: The factor eps is to prevent division by zero, and the value is chosen to match that used in [[SMPTE-ST-2094-50]](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-color-hdr/#biblio-smpte-st-2094-50).

As a draft text of SMPTE-ST-2094-50 has recently been [released to the public](https://github.com/SMPTE/st2094-50) I had a look and am unable to find a factor called `eps` or the value `0.001`

@ccameron-chromium could you help me out with a section number or equation number?
