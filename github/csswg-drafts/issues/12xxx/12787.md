Should `column-height:0` really be allowed? If yes, then https://drafts.csswg.org/css-break/#breaking-rules at least makes the *columns* at least 1px tall, but not the lines. Maybe that's fine?

@rachelandrew

## @rachelandrew — 2025-09-12

I'm not sure `column-height: 0` makes any sense, but making it at least 1px tall if that does happen seems reasonable.

<!-- comment id=3290619131 author=frivoal created=2025-09-15T06:08:19Z url=https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/issues/12787#issuecomment-3290619131 -->
## @frivoal — 2025-09-15

I don't think a column-height of 0 is of any use, but I don't know if there's precedent for using a `]0, +∞]` open interval for lengths. Open intervals are annoying in that since we don't define the precision, the smallest valid value is implementation dependent, and that's not great. We can work around that by setting a 1px minimum, but then again, why 1px specifically? Why not 1pt, or 1ch, or 0.1ch… it's very arbitrary.

Unless there's precedent or a clever solution I cannot think of, I suspect we should just let it start at 0 at the syntax level, and use a minimum size in terms of actual layout computation (`1px` will do fine, it's arbitrary, but that bothers me a lot less in layout than in parsing).

<!-- comment id=3293385604 author=fantasai created=2025-09-15T18:16:42Z url=https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/issues/12787#issuecomment-3293385604 -->
## @fantasai — 2025-09-15

+1 to @frivoal's point about open intervals. Also we already have a precedent for forcing things to be 1px when we need a non-zero size: `column-width` floors at 1px, and `repeat(auto-fit)` in Grid floors the effective track size at 1px when trying to calculate how many tracks can fit.
