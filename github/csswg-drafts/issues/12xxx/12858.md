## @alanbaradlay — 2026-09-04

> IIRC WebKit tried to alias this over this spring, but ran into some issues and had to revert, see [WebKit/WebKit@8b5352b](https://github.com/WebKit/WebKit/commit/8b5352bbeb711bdfd7a273503abdf543818abdae) CC [@alanbaradlay](https://github.com/alanbaradlay) who probably remembers the details.

`stretch` consults the containing block while `fill-available` climbs the containing block chain until an ancestor with definite block size is found (it's the classic percent height resolve quirk vs. standards behavior).

<!-- comment id=5544033254 author=bfgeek created=2026-09-04T17:15:56Z url=https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/issues/12858#issuecomment-5544033254 -->
## @bfgeek — 2026-09-04

Right we've shipped it as an alias. We worked over the course of a few years (2019-2024ish) to slowly shift `-webkit-fill-available` to how stretch was specified to work. The final aliasing change for us was minimal as the behaviour differences really was only the ignoring margins thing.
