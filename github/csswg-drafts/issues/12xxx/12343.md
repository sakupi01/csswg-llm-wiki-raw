[The spec](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-inline-3/#valdef-initial-letter-wrap-length) for `<length>` and `<percentage>` units in `initial-letter-wrap` has this issue:
"ISSUE 25 These values and related annoyance is likely unnecessary if someone submits a patch to Blink to support ['first'](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-inline-3/#valdef-initial-letter-wrap-first)."

Why is this tied to the completeness of the Blink implementation specifically?
Firefox doesn't have this property at all currently and I'm not sure about Safari, but it seems odd to tie the existence / removal of a feature to what happens to not (yet) be supported in a specific implementation.

I just feel like there's context missing here and it confuses me greatly.

The [commit message](https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/commit/f84385e1dd7d2d9b2aa7c9555f50cf972c327a3b) for this also does not help at all.

## @Shrinivassab — 2025-06-18

Hi @Psychpsyo  Thanks for raising this. I have also been reviewing [Issue #12343](https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/issues/12343), and I share the confusion around the note in the spec:

“ISSUE 25 These values and related annoyance is likely unnecessary if someone submits a patch to Blink to support 'first'.”

It does seem odd to tie the relevance of <length> and <percentage> units for initial-letter-wrap to the Blink implementation specifically, especially when other engines (e.g., Firefox) don not support the property at all yet. The relationship between Blink’s implementation of 'first' and the broader spec behavior isn’t clearly explained.

Could someone point to the original discussion or rationale behind ISSUE 25? The commit message that introduced it doesn’t provide much context.

Depending on that background, potential resolutions might include:

- Rephrasing the issue note to reflect a broader interop concern rather than a single browser’s state.

- Adding an editorial comment in the spec to capture the historical rationale.

- Removing the issue note if the original concern is no longer applicable.

Happy to help with edits or a PR once there's more clarity. Thanks!
