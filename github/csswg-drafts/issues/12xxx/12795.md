We've decided to base `line-clamp` on `continue: collapse`, and this is where there's current implementation interest. At the same time, `continue: discard` remains more versatile, and albeit at a lower priority, we're still exploring that option.

I think we should continue to do so, but presenting them on the same footing does a disservice to the reader of the spec, as one is clearly more short term. Given that implementation interest is more focused on `collapse` that `discard`, I think we should mark `discard` at risk.
