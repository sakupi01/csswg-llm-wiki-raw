## @tabatkins — 2021-04-28

:playing and :paused are mutually exclusive, and every media element matches *exactly one* of them at all times. :buffering and :stalled are subsets of :playing. These are already indicated by the spec.

@hober indicated in the call that she believes :seeking is independent of :playing and :paused; an element can be in either state while seeking. This makes sense to me, it just needs some clarification in the spec.
