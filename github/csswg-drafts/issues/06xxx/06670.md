## @noamr — 2021-10-16

> @noamr @tabatkins If that change looks good, I think we can close the issue.

I think that edit is incorrect, if I understand the original meaning of "origin" that was there.
I believe it should point to [`[=cascade origin=]`](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-cascade-5/#origin) and not to `[=url/origin=]` (which is the source of the original confusion).

When imported stylesheets come from a different origin they don't automatically receive the URL origin of their importer, that would be a same-origin policy violation. Cascade origin means something different, less about URL origins.
