## @SelenIT — 2022-07-04

According to the "Drawing the Border Image" [section](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-backgrounds-3/#border-image-process),

> If the first keyword is [repeat](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-backgrounds-3/#valdef-border-image-repeat-repeat), the top, middle, and bottom images are centered horizontally in their respective areas. ...
> 
> If the second keyword is [repeat](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-backgrounds-3/#valdef-border-image-repeat-repeat), the left, middle, and right images are centered vertically in their respective areas.

I believe that the behavior of Firefox (non-centering) is a bug.
