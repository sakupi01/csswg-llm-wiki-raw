Inter-character bopomofo annotations are go on the **right** side of the character they annotate. We wanted to confirm if that's the correct answer in bidi situations, or if it should be the inline-end side.

(Given that right-to-left Chinese is a thing that exists, and it would almost certainly also place annotations on the right side, our inclination is to keep this definition as placement on the right.)
