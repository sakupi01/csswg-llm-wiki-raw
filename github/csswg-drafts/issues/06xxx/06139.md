The [list-style-image](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-lists-3/#image-markers) property is specified with a `discrete` animation type. However, its type, `<image>`, [supports interpolation](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-images-4/#interpolation) as of css-images-4. Per the results for [css/css-lists/animations/list-style-image-interpolation.html](https://wpt.fyi/results/css/css-lists/animations/list-style-image-interpolation.html) it appears Chrome and Safari both support interpolation for this property already.
