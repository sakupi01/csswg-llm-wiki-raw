[This footnote](https://www.w3.org/TR/mediaqueries-4/#ref-for-valdef-media-speech%E2%91%A2) under the "Changes Since Media Queries Level 3" still mentions speech as a remaining media type, but in the current level 4 TR it's deprecated, as mentioned in [this footnote](https://www.w3.org/TR/mediaqueries-4/#ref-for-valdef-media-speech%E2%91%A1). The former footnote should be amended.
