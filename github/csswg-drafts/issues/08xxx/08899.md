Both an `index.html` and an `Overview.html` file exist for Editor's Drafts published under `drafts.csswg.org`. The `index.html` seems to be the most up-to-date.

For example:

- https://drafts.csswg.org/css-animations-1/ - dated 12 May 2023
- https://drafts.csswg.org/css-animations-1/index.html - dated 12 May 2023
- https://drafts.csswg.org/css-animations-1/Overview.html - dated 2 March 2023

I don't know if anything links to the URL with a filename. Maybe not. That said, I think that only the `Overview.html` used to exist in the past. Should the `Overview.html` version now be seen as deprecated? Or was the switch to `index.html` unintended?
