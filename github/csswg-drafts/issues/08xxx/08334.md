https://drafts.csswg.org/css-grid-2/#grid-order

> Grid-modified document order (grid order) is the order in which [grid items](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-grid-2/#grid-item) are encountered when traversing the grid’s [grid cells](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-grid-2/#grid-cell). If two items are encountered at the same time, they are taken in [order-modified document order](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-display-4/#order-modified-document-order).

The definition should clarify that references to this term are supposed to specify the major axis, explain what the major axis is, and probably have a diagram similar to this one from https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Row-_and_column-major_order by CMG Lee ([CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/deed.en), no changes made)
