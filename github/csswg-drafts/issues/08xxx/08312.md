As far as I can see, currently in all browsers, the [system colors](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-color-4/#css-system-colors) resolve to sRGB.

But going forward that might not be the case, and requiring gamut mapping or clamping to the sRGB gamut seems undesirable.

in [Resolving other colors](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-color-4/#resolving-other-colors) the spec tries to look forwards here, with a somewhat aspirational

> Each [<system-color>](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-color-4/#typedef-system-color) keyword computes to the corresponding color in its color space. However, such colors must not be altered by 'forced colors mode'.

In contrast the sections on [resolving](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-color-4/#resolving-sRGB-values) and [serializing](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-color-4/#serializing-sRGB-values) correspond to current reality, in that the computed value of a system color is an sRGB color which is required to serialize in the legacy, comma-separated `rgb()` or `rgba()` form which also uses a 0.0 to 255.0 range, has a minimum 8-bit precision, and thus cannot represent out-of-sRGB-gamut colors.
