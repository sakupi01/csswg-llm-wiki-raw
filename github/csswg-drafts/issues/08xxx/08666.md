`scroll-snap-type` uses `x` and `y`, but `scroll-timeline-axis` uses `horizontal` and `vertical`. We should get these aligned. :)

<!-- comment id=1494910564 author=fantasai created=2023-04-03T20:06:06Z url=https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/issues/8666#issuecomment-1494910564 -->
## @fantasai — 2023-04-03

Proposal: rename `horizontal` and `vertical` keywords in Scroll Animations to `x` and `y` to match Scroll Snap (`scroll-snap-type`) and Overflow (`overflow-x/y`).
