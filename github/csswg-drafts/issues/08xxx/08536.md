We now have [text-space-trim](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-text-4/#white-space-trim) and [text-spacing-trim](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-text-4/#text-spacing-trim-property) in css-text-4. They accomplish different goals, but have similar names. It might be useful to rename one of them, e.g., change `text-space-trim` to `white-space-trim`?
