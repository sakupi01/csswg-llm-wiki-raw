It is not clear to me why these [`@page` properties](https://drafts.csswg.org/css2/#the-page) defined in CSS2 are not listed in [`@page` property list](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-page-3/#page-property-list) from CSS Paged Media 3.

Aside: I use the term "property" but "descriptor" might be more appropriate.

## @faceless2 — 2023-02-08

Those don't apply in @page. They're regular properties and are defined in css-break. The two sections are simply adjacent in https://drafts.csswg.org/css2/#the-page

## @cdoublev — 2023-02-08

After reading more carefully their definitions in CSS 2, I now understand that they are used in a print view but only apply on elements, not in `@page` or margin rules.

Thanks for the clarification.
